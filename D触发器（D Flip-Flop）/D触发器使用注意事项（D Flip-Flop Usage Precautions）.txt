【设计声明】
本项目的 D 触发器逻辑架构基于网络经典的主从 D 触发器（NAND 门结构）进行参考，但所有的晶体管级物理实现、PCB 布局走线、电源分配以及踩坑排雷，均为作者独立完成。

Design Statement: The logic architecture of this D flip-flop is based on the classic master-slave D flip-flop (NAND gate structure) found online. However, all transistor-level physical implementations, PCB layouts, power distribution, and debugging processes are independently completed by the author.


D触发器使用注意事项 / D Flip-Flop Usage Precautions
【模块组成】 / [Module Composition]
纯分立元件（2N3904 + 1N4148）手搓 D 触发器，带复位功能。
Hand-built D Flip-Flop using pure discrete components (2N3904 + 1N4148), with Reset function.

【使用注意事项】 / [Usage Notes]

VCC 接 5V，GND 接地。
Connect VCC to 5V, GND to Ground.

D 接数据，CP 接时钟（按键），Reset 接复位。
Connect D to Data, CP to Clock (push button), Reset to Reset.

Q 和 Q' 是输出。
Q and Q' are outputs.

V1.0：初代版本，因复位逻辑存在致命短路缺陷，成为废案。
V1.0: Initial version. Scrapped due to a fatal short-circuit flaw in the reset logic.

V2.0：V1.0 的补线抢救版，但后期复盘发现依然存在设计漏洞。
V2.0: A wire-fix version of V1.0. However, further review revealed remaining design flaws.

【容易踩坑的点】 / [Common Pitfalls]

1. 复位线（Reset）必须接好。
The Reset line MUST be properly connected.

结果：悬空或者接错地，数据会直接锁死在 0，按什么都没反应。

Result: If it's floating or connected to the wrong ground, the data will be locked at 0, and pressing anything will do nothing.

2. 时钟线（CP）绝对不能悬空！
The Clock line (CP) must NEVER be left floating!

结果：如果你把时钟线拔了，或者悬空放那儿，Q 端会输出 5.1V，但 Q' 端会给你搞出一个 3.9V 的变态半高电平。

Result: If you unplug the clock line or leave it floating, the Q output will be 5.1V, but the Q' output will give you a weird 3.9V half-high level.

注意：时钟切换的时候，必须接 VCC 或者拉回 GND，千万别让悬空在空中！否则会在 Q' 端出现幽灵电压，比如我的测量值就是不高不低的 3.9V。

Note: When switching the clock, it must be connected to VCC or pulled back to GND. Never let it float in the air! Otherwise, a "ghost voltage" will appear at the Q' terminal (e.g., my measured value was exactly that awkward 3.9V).

3. 漏连导线（飞线抢救）。
Missing wire connection (Flying wire rescue).

结果：V1.0 版本因为画图少连了一根线，直接不工作。

Result: V1.0 didn't work at all because I missed connecting one wire in the schematic.

怎么办：拿根线飞线硬连上去，板子就活了，但是我给出了 V2.0，不使用 1.0 就可以了，但是依然有重大缺陷。
Solution: Hardwired it with a flying wire and the board came alive. However, I released V2.0, so you can just ignore V1.0. But V2.0 still has major flaws.

4.重制D触发器核心，用时一下午，推出V3.0双核版本，是目前的稳定版。
Refactored the D Flip-Flop core in just one afternoon and rolled out V3.0 (Dual-Core), which is now the current stable version.

踩坑Number one：
在制作D触发器的时候没有加复位功能，所以那几块D触发器的PCB成为废板了！只能当演示工具用了。
(Pitfall No. 1: I forgot to add a reset function when building the D flip-flops, so those PCBs became scrap boards and can now only be used for demonstration.)

踩坑Number two：
在绘制PC核心的时候忘记加自加器（增量器），幸好在画图的时候发现了，但是还是废了一张草图。
(Pitfall No. 2: I forgot to add the incrementer when designing the PC core. Fortunately, I caught it during drafting, but it still wasted one sketch.)

踩坑Number three：
突然发现2N3904不能完全替代2N3906！一个适合拉到高电平，一个适合拉到低电平！一个是PNP一个是NPN，我有一个好记的方法，P喜欢进攻，N容易被进攻，所以PNP中基极是输出电流的，而NPN的基极是输入电流的。
(Pitfall No. 3: I suddenly realized that a 2N3904 (NPN) cannot completely replace a 2N3906 (PNP)! One is suitable for pulling HIGH, and the other for pulling LOW. I have a good mnemonic: P likes to attack (source), while N is easily attacked (sink). In a PNP transistor, current flows OUT of the base; in an NPN, it flows INTO the base.)

踩坑Number four:
理论加法器，也就是网上流传的那种没有注意三极管压降，如果是四个加法器连在一起的话就会出现问题！最后那个加法器输出1产生的电压可能连LED都驱动不了！
(Pitfall No. 4: Theoretical adders—like the ones commonly found online—ignore transistor voltage drops. If four adders are cascaded, problems will occur! The voltage produced when the final adder outputs a logic '1' might be too low to even drive an LED!)

踩坑Number five:
门电路的输出端应该在三极管的集电极，最好不要在发射级，因为这样可能会有电压拉不高的问题。
(The output of a logic gate should be taken from the collector of the transistor, not the emitter, as this might cause issues with the voltage not pulling up high enough.)

踩坑Number six:
当使用多个分立元件门电路的时候，要注意考虑上一级的上拉或下拉电阻的影响，不然会让整体瘫痪！
(When using multiple discrete logic gates, you must consider the impact of the previous stage's pull-up or pull-down resistors. Otherwise, the whole system will crash!)
